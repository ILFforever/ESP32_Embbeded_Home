Dark is not an inversion

three real differences

Depth moves to the edge

On a dark ground the shadow stack barely reads. The bevel highlight goes up in relative weight and the fills come down, so the rim does the work the cast shadow does in light.

The veil is tinted, not white

White at low alpha over dark grey goes milky and hides the wallpaper it exists to reveal. Dark glass is rgba(56,64,76,.5) — a tinted dark veil.

The wallpaper needs pigment

Pale washes vanish on dark, so the six colour fields become jewel tones at lower opacity. Without them the panes have nothing behind them and read as flat boxes.
